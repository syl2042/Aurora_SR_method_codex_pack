# Aurora approved patterns

Capture validated product patterns so other apps can reuse them without copying entire screens.

## Promote a pattern when

- the human approved it;
- it solves a recurring product problem;
- it appears or is likely to appear in multiple pages/apps;
- its behavior can be expressed independently of one product's data model.

## Pattern record

For each approved pattern, store:

- name;
- problem solved;
- target personas;
- information hierarchy;
- interaction behavior;
- progressive disclosure levels;
- component implementation;
- responsive behavior;
- examples/screens where approved;
- anti-patterns/when not to use it.

## Examples of candidate families

- workspace header;
- source list/explorer;
- document detail;
- technical diagnostics drawer;
- status rail;
- creation flow;
- AI answer with evidence/citations;
- agent/run inspection;
- settings with advanced disclosure;
- multi-format generation studio.

A pattern is not a screenshot to clone. It is a reusable product decision.
